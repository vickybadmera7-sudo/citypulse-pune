Quick safety tips

  • Prefer busy, well-lit roads after dark and share your live location with someone you trust.
  • Avoid underpasses and low-lying roads during heavy rain.
  • Use prepaid autos/cabs at the railway station and bus stands.
  • Emergency numbers (India): 112 (all emergencies), 100 (police), 108 (ambulance), 1091 (women helpline).
